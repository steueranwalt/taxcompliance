“Give me an intercompany agreement for a contract manufacturing arrangement.”

“Yes, sure, what kind of arrangement is it?”

  


Intra-group contract manufacturing arrangements can take a number of different legal and commercial forms, each of which may correspond to a very different functional profile and risk profile.

  


The slide below shows three of the most common options.

  


Relevant questions to ask include:

* What’s the chain of legal title from the procurement of raw materials to the supply of finished products to third party customers?

* What’s the chain of possession and physical delivery? Where are stocks of raw materials and finished products physically held?

* Which risks are intended to be borne by the manufacturer? (e.g. market risk, price of raw materials, inventory risk re raw materials & finished products, FX risks, product liability risks etc)

* What is the manufacturing entity’s capacity to bear the relevant risks and control those risks?

* What are the legal rights comprising the existing intangibles relating to the manufacturing process and the product design (including registered IP and also unregistered know-how)?

* Which entity currently owns which of those rights?

* Which entity should own the legal rights in improvements in those respective intangibles?

* Is the manufacturer guaranteed a minimum return? In what circumstances would it be appropriate for the manufacturer to make a loss?

* What is the intended contractual mechanism for setting prices or other payments, and for making post-year-end true up or true down adjustments?
