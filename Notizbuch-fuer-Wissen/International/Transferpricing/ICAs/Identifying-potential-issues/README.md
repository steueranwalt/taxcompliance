STEPS FOR ENGAGING WITH THE LEGAL FUNCTION

  


1\. Explain the issue and the business case for taking action

  


2\. Explain the end result required and the deadlines involved

  


3\. Assess existing legal capacity, skills and resources

  


4\. Build the team and identify the stakeholders

  


5\. Plan and implement an appropriate approach

  


6\. Keep the legal team informed and updated
