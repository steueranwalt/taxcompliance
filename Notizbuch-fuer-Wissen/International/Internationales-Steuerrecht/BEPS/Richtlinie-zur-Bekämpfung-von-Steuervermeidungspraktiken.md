# Richtlinie zur Bekämpfung von Steuervermeidungspraktiken 

The anti-avoidance measures in the [Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive ](<http://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv:OJ.L_.2016.193.01.0001.01.ENG&toc=OJ:L:2016:193:TOC>)(EU) 2016/1164 other than the rule on hybrid mismatches, are:

---|---  
Switchover rule: to prevent double non-taxation of certain income. | [/taxation_customs/file/11986_deviolet_4.png](<https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/sites/taxation/files/violet_4.png>) [ ![](https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/siteCollections/obenhaus.sharepoint.com,c8e1905c-859d-437a-be04-8a06fe606322,df8e44e5-a0c7-49d4-a950-711142afe01e/onenote/resources/1-2ba4ae12216500ee0ef51cc892c7d42e!1-d1b56193-925a-41d4-a35e-ff62d4b37978/$value) ](<https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/sites/taxation/files/violet_4.png>)  
Exit taxation: to prevent companies from avoiding tax when re-locating assets. | [/taxation_customs/file/11987_deviolet_5.png](<https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/sites/taxation/files/violet_5.png>) [ ![](https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/siteCollections/obenhaus.sharepoint.com,c8e1905c-859d-437a-be04-8a06fe606322,df8e44e5-a0c7-49d4-a950-711142afe01e/onenote/resources/1-01c9b853e39c00710b5b60273b0be425!1-d1b56193-925a-41d4-a35e-ff62d4b37978/$value) ](<https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/sites/taxation/files/violet_5.png>)
