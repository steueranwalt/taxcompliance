# MNE’s Intercompany Agreements

Streamlining an MNE’s Intercompany Agreements:

What Works and What Doesn’t Work

  
  
  


It’s not uncommon that we come across groups with disproportionately voluminous portfolios of intercompany agreements.

  


We also see groups with incredibly complicated allocation keys which have arisen over a period of years and which have no justification from a TP perspective.

  


Yes, it is important to document the legal and commercial terms of intercompany transactions.

  


But unnecessary complexity doesn’t help anyone.

  


In the slide below are some do’s and don’ts for streamlining a group’s portfolio of intercompany agreements, to eliminate unnecessary costs and risk of error.
